rke2_node_drain now runs before rke2_prepare

Rationalerke2_prepare reboots the node. Rebooting before draining hard-kills every pod on a node that is still carrying its full workload, and the drain then has to move those same pods anyway, doubling the disruption.
DetailsReordered in both the server and agent plays of upgrade_rke2.yml. Observed on umoja zewditu, whose ~48 pods were killed by the reboot before the drain even started.
